      How this was put together. The active register is IT Inventory V03, which already carries the cleaned-up holder names listed on its Owner Changes sheet; both spreadsheets were otherwise read as-is. Funder and supplier names spelt in different ways were merged (for example “Unicef” and “UNICEF”, or five spellings of Step Forward). Owners are grouped into custody types; the Arabic entry for the health units of the Youth Role project in Sohag and Assiut is shown as a partner facility. Blank, “0” and “*” values are shown as “Not recorded”. No items were matched between the two registers: their tag numbers and serials do not overlap. All items on the old IT items list are treated as for sale.
